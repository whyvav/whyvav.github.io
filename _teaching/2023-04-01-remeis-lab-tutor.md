Supervised BSc Physics students undertaking their astronomical internship at the Dr. Karl Remeis-Sternwarte Bamberg. The experiment covered the characteristics of optical charge-coupled devices (CCDs) and their post-analysis application to imaging of real astronomical objects.

Responsibilities included guiding students through CCD calibration procedures, data reduction, and image analysis, as well as supporting the broader lab and research environment at the observatory.

**Duration:** April 2023 &ndash; October 2024
