**BSc Final Project** &ensp; | &ensp; Aug 2019 &ndash; Jan 2020 &ensp; | &ensp; Modern College, Pune (SPPU)

Surveyed visualization methods for special relativity with an emphasis on computerized simulations, and implemented a virtual-camera model in a Unity game engine to demonstrate relativistic effects including Lorentz contraction, time dilation, and relativistic aberration of light.

**Tools:** Unity, C#, Computational Physics, Scientific Visualization
