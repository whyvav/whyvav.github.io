Developing a scalable multimodal machine learning framework for automated detection and classification of bubble-like ISM structures &mdash; including supernova remnants, HII regions, stellar wind bubbles, and planetary nebulae &mdash; by fine-tuning open-weight vision-language models on multiwavelength survey data. The goal is to produce reproducible detection pipelines and publishable ISM catalogues.

Targeting *Astronomy & Astrophysics* and a proceedings contribution to ADASS XXXVI.
