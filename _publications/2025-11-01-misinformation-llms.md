Co-authored a study developing an auditor&ndash;node framework to simulate and quantify how biases, ideologies, and cognitive heuristics drive misinformation evolution and factual degradation across networks of persona-LLMs.

Presented at the [1st Workshop on LLM Agents for Social Simulation (LASS)](https://lass-workshop.github.io/) at [ACM CIKM 2025](https://cikm2025.org/). Preprint at [arXiv:2511.10384](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.10384). Awarded the **LASS Outstanding Paper Award**. Under review at *Journal of Computer Science and Technology* (JCST) after first revision.
