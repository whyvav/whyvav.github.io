Presented MSc thesis research on the X-ray evolution of supernova remnants in the Large Magellanic Cloud at the ["junge AG" Summer School](https://astronomische-gesellschaft.de/jag25/view_abstract.php?id=413) organized by the Astronomische Gesellschaft. The talk covered the spectral analysis of LMC SNR J0500-6512 and the population study of the complete observed LMC SNR sample.
