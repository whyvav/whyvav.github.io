Presented the paper and poster "An Empirical Exploration of Socioeconomic Linguistic Bias in Text-to-Image Generation" at the [Student Research Workshop](https://2026.eacl.org/calls/srw/) of [EACL 2026](https://2026.eacl.org/). The paper demonstrates measurable socioeconomic bias in text-to-image models, showing they tend to generate lighter skin tones for high-income occupations.
